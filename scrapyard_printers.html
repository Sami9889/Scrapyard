Scrapyard Printers

Build a real 3D printer almost entirely from junk. You get $250 and strict limits—everything else must be scavenged from e-waste, scrapyards, and flea markets.

Get Started on Slack ➔

The Core Rules

$250 Grant

Buy New (Strict)

You are ONLY allowed to buy these components brand new:

  • Motherboard
  • Raspberry Pi
  • Extruder
  • GT2 Belts
  • Max 10m Aluminium Extrusion
Junk Only

Scavenge The Rest

Everything else must come from used/salvaged sources:

  • eBay Used Listings
  • Thrift Shops & Yard Sales
  • E-Waste Piles & Scrapyards
  • Flea Markets & Old Scanners
Custom CAD

Print Missing Parts

Can't find a specific bracket, mount, or custom piece?

  • 3D model your own custom parts
  • Print them on a friend's printer
  • Hack together custom mounts
