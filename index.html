Scrapyard Printers

Build a working 3D printer mostly from junk. You get $250 for a handful of new parts; everything else has to come from scrapyards, e-waste, thrift shops, or flea markets.

Join Slack Channel RSVP Now

The Core Rules

$250 Grant

Buy New (Strict)

These are the only parts you can buy new with your $250:

  • Motherboard
  • Raspberry Pi
  • Extruder
  • GT2 Belts
  • Max 10m Aluminium Extrusion
Junk Only

Scavenge The Rest

For everything else, hit up these kinds of sources:

  • eBay Used Listings
  • Thrift Shops & Yard Sales
  • E-Waste Piles & Scrapyards
  • Flea Markets & Old Scanners
Custom CAD

Print Missing Parts

Can't find a bracket or custom mount? Model it yourself and print it on someone else's printer.

  • 3D model your own custom parts
  • Print them on a friend's printer
  • Hack together custom mounts

The Rewards

Travel Grant

Gold Coast Trip

Put in 40 hours of building and documenting your frankenstein machine, and Hack Club will fly you out to the Gold Coast!

Grand Prize

50kg of Filament

The most creative, functional scrapyard printer built by a participant wins 50kg of filament—enough to keep printing for a long time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Keep a build log—photos, video updates, CAD files, and notes on your progress. Hack Club uses WakaTime or simple scrapbooking tools for this.
Ask a friend, hit up a local makerspace, use a school printer, or post in the Slack channel—someone will usually help out.
Snap photos of your thrift shop hauls, screenshot eBay receipts marked 'Used/For Parts', or film yourself pulling steppers out of an old 3D printer. You also need to take a photo of the actual item and the receipt together. That's the proof.
